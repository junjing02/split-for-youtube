Install in a minute.

It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it into your browser yourself. It takes five steps.

Download ZIP
  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the split-for-youtube folder.
  5. Open any YouTube video.

The split turns on in a large, landscape window. In smaller windows, YouTube keeps its normal layout.

Questions, answered.

Is it free? Is my data safe?

Yes, it's completely free. It runs entirely in your browser, collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium-based browsers. Firefox and Safari aren't supported.

Why don't I see the split layout?

The split needs a large, landscape window, so make the window bigger or go full screen. It also steps aside in theater mode; press t to switch back.

Does it work with live streams?

Yes. While a stream is live, the Recommended pane shows the live chat instead.

How do I turn on ambient light?

Switch YouTube to its dark theme, then turn on Ambient mode in the player's settings (the gear icon). The glow follows that setting. You can adjust its strength, or turn it off, from the extension's toolbar icon.

Can I change how it works?

Yes. Click the extension's toolbar icon to switch the split on or off, use two windows, put the video on the right, or have it remember your layout between videos. Double-click a divider to reset it.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Please open an issue on GitHub. There's a short form for bug reports and one for feature ideas.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the extension's icon in the toolbar and choose Remove, or click Remove on its card in chrome://extensions. YouTube goes straight back to normal.
